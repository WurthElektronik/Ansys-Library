`Ansys Libraries for Würth Elektronik Products`
=========
## Introduction
Welcome to the Würth Elektronik Ansys Library repository. <br>
The libraries are created and maintained by the Würth Elektronik.<br>
For any questions or suggestions, please feel free to contact the team at:  <libraries@we-online.com>

## Library Component Coverage
* RF Antennas <br>
_WE-MCA_

* RF Inductors <br>
_WE-ACHC, WE-MK, WE-KIHC, WE-KI, WE-TCI, WE-CAIR_

* Filter Chokes <br>
_WE-SD_

* EMC Shielding <br>
_WE-FAS_RFID, WE-FAS_EMI, WE-SHC, WE-SHC_Seamless, WE-FSFS_

* Heatsinks <br>
_WE-HICI, WE-HTOI, WE-HTO, WE-HIC_

* Power Magnetics <br>
_WE-HCIM, WE-CSIM_

* RF Capacitors <br>
_WCAP-CSRF_

* SMA Connectors <br>
_WR-SMA End Launch, WR-SMA Panel Flange, WR-SMA THT-THR-SMT, WR-SMA Solderless_

* Thermal Management <br>
_WE-TTT, WE-TGS, WE-TGFG, WE-PCM, WE-TINS, WE-TGF_
